For programming background music, here are some suggestions that many developers find helpful:

**Electronic/Ambient:**
- **Lofi Hip Hop** - The classic "lofi hip hop radio - beats to relax/study to" streams are popular for a reason
- **Synthwave/Retrowave** - Artists like HOME, Com Truise, or the "Outrun" genre
- **Ambient Electronic** - Carbon Based Lifeforms, Solar Fields, or Boards of Canada
- **Chillstep** - Blackmill, Phaeleh, or CMA

**Classical/Orchestral:**
- **Minimalist Classical** - Max Richter, Ólafur Arnalds, or Nils Frahm
- **Film Scores** - Hans Zimmer, Thomas Newman, or the Interstellar soundtrack
- **Piano** - Ludovico Einaudi or Yiruma

**Game Soundtracks:**
- **Minecraft** - C418's soundtrack is surprisingly good for focus
- **Civilization VI** - Excellent orchestral pieces
- **Ori and the Blind Forest** - Beautiful and atmospheric
- **FTL: Faster Than Light** - Great electronic soundtrack

**Nature/White Noise:**
- Rain sounds or thunderstorms
- Coffee shop ambience
- [Brain.fm](https://brain.fm) or [Noisli](https://www.noisli.com) for scientifically designed focus music

The key is finding something without lyrics (or in a language you don't understand) that maintains a consistent energy level without being distracting. What kind of music do you usually gravitate toward?
